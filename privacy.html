1. 모으는 정보: 없음

유령 과수원은 개발자의 서버로 어떤 정보도 보내지 않습니다. 계정도 없습니다.

애플 스크린타임 기능으로 정한 밤 시간에 고른 앱을 가리고, 그 밤에 폰을 1분 넘게 썼는지만 확인합니다. 애플은 앱에 어떤 앱을 썼는지 알려 주지 않으므로, 유령 과수원은 어떤 앱을 썼는지 · 무엇을 봤는지 알 수 없습니다.

Ghost Orchard Privacy Policy

Effective: November 9, 2026

1. We collect nothing

Ghost Orchard sends no information to the developer's servers. There are no accounts.

2. What stays on your phone

  • Your night window and settings (such as strict mode)
  • Each night's result (kept, over 1 minute, or unknown), when you went over 1 minute and roughly how long (over 5, 15, 30 or 60 minutes)
  • Your harvests and which screens you have already seen

Deleting the app deletes all of it.

3. Screen Time

The app uses Apple's Screen Time features to cover the apps you pick during your night window and to check whether the phone was used for more than 1 minute that night. Apple does not tell the app which apps you used, so Ghost Orchard cannot see which apps you used or what you looked at.

4. Notifications

The reminder 10 minutes before night is scheduled on your phone and does not go through any server.

5. Third parties

No ads, no analytics, no external services. When you tap Share, the image and text go to the system share sheet and you choose where they go.

6. Changes

Changes will be posted on this page and in the update notes.
