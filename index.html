Ghost Orchard Support

I need an app at night

Tap "Open for 1 minute" on the cover screen and it opens right away. If you go past 1 minute that night, only that night doesn't grow. We suggest leaving alarm, authenticator, banking and health apps out of the covered apps. Calls always work.

Emergency in strict mode

Open Ghost Orchard and tap "Emergency unlock". Apps stay uncovered until morning. Once a week.

Settings are locked at night

This is on purpose, so the night can't be ended early by changing the time. You can change them after the night ends.

What is a striped square?

A night the app couldn't watch because Screen Time was off. It doesn't count as a miss.
